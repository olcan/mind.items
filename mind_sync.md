#mind_sync is the vault's mind sync: the vault-side loop (`bin/mind_sync_loop.py` over `bin/mind_sync.py` in the vault repo; runbook `docs/mind_sync.md`, designs `notes/design/mind_sync.md` and `notes/design/mind_sync_loop.md`) mirrors the vault's agent files as editable `#vault/…` items rendered by [template/vault](#template/vault), applies the pulls, pushes, creates, and retirements it can decide on its own, and delivers what it cannot decide as a hold: a [todo](#todoer) item whose first line is `#todo mind sync: <cause> <subject> — <remedy> #_mind_sync <key>` (the fixed remedy is the action; the diagnostic detail is the inert region below it). When the hold clears, the loop completes the item (`#todo` to `#done` on the first line) only if it is unchanged and not snoozed; an edited, snoozed, or deleted item is left alone, and a completed item is never deleted (delete it yourself). The loop's tag is hidden, and a hidden tag is a dependency that needs one uniquely labelled item: this item, a plain description with no code, is that dependency (without it every hold item is a red-bordered missing dependency). The loop's record, health file, and log live on the vault host under `states/mindpage_sync/` and `logs/mind_sync_loop/`; pause it with `touch states/mindpage_sync/loop.pause` (wait for the `paused` log line) before any attended sync operation.
